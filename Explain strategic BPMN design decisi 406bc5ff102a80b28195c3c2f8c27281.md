# Strategic BPMN Design Decisions

For our Bristol Regional Food Network project, I created a strategic BPMN model to show the main business processes and how different participants interact with the marketplace.

I used separate pools for the Producer, Customer, Marketplace System, Organic Certification Verification Service and External Payment Service. This helps show the responsibilities of each participant. Within the Marketplace, I used two lanes to separate Marketplace Operations from Quality and Sustainability activities.

I used subprocesses to represent the main business activities, including product management, customer ordering, payment processing, order fulfilment, food quality and sustainability. Since this is a strategic-level model, I focused on the overall business rather than showing every small step.

I used solid sequence flows to show the order of activities within a process and dashed message flows to show communication between different participants. For example, producers send product information to the marketplace, and the marketplace communicates with the external certification service to verify organic claims.

Our group selected Product Listing and Validation, and Inventory and Seasonal Availability Management as the two main functionalities for Use Case 1. These are represented within the Product Management and Inventory Control subprocess and will be developed further in Sprint 2.
