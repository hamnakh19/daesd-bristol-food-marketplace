
# Sprint 1 Plan- Strategic Process Analysis and Project Setup

## Sprint 1 Goal

The goal of Sprint 1 was to understand and analyse the Local Food Marketplace case study, focusing on Use Case 1: Producer Listing and Inventory Management. Our group aims to identify the major business processes and organisational responsibilities, develop a strategic-level BPMN model, select and scope two candidate major functionalities, and establish project management and version-control practices.

## Team Responsibilities

| Member | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Hamna | Sprint Planning, backlog, Kanban board |
| Khadija | Strategic BPMN modelling |
| Tomi | Business process and organisational analysis |
| Tahiyah | Functionality selection, scope and justification |

## Planned deliverables

- Strategic BPMN model of the enterprise
- Analysis of major business processes and organisational responsibilities
- Two proposed major functionalities with clear scope and justification
- Initial product backlog
- Sprint 1 Kanban board
- Risk register
- Github repository containing development evidence
- Evidence of individual contributions

## Progress Tracking

Our group decided on using Notion to maintain the product backlog, assign responsibilities and track progress using a Kanban board. The sprint 1 tasks are categorised by status, allowing members to record work that is not started, in progress or completed. A separate contribution view group tasks by assignee to provide visibility of individual responsibilities. Github is used to store project and maintain version-control evidence.

## Task Prioritisation

Sprint 1 tasks were prioritised according to the assessment requirements and their importance to the project's development.

| Priority | Tasks | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| High | Strategic BPMN model | Represents the overall enterprise and forms the foundation for subsequent modelling |
| High | Business process analysis | Identifies the processes, actors and responsibilities needed for accurate modelling |
| High | Functionality selection and scoping | Establishes the two major functionalities to be developed in later sprints |
| High | Product backlog and sprint planning | Ensures deliverables, responsibilities and progress are organised |
| High | GitHub repository and development evidence | Supports version control and verification of individual contributions |
| Medium | Risk Register and contribution visualisation | Supports risk awareness, collaboration and progress monitoring |

The strategic BPMN, business analysis and functionality selection were treated as key deliverables because they establish the foundation for Sprint 2.

## Task Dependencies

Some Sprint 1 activities depend on the outcomes of other tasks.

- Business process analysis informs the development and review of the strategic BPMN.
- The strategic analysis supports the identification and scoping of the two major functionalities.
- The product backlog and Kanban board support the allocation and monitoring of individual tasks.
- GitHub provides a central repository for storing completed deliverables and maintaining development evidence.

These dependencies help the group understand how individual contributions support the overall project.

## Changes to Task Allocation

During the initial stages of Sprint 1, some responsibilities overlapped as group members worked on understanding and analysing the case study.

Following our group meeting on 27 October 2026, we reviewed our task allocation and redistributed responsibilities more clearly.

Tomi took responsibility for the final business process analysis, Khadija for strategic BPMN modelling, Tahiyah for functionality selection and scoping, and Hamna for sprint planning and project management.

The revised responsibilities were recorded in Notion to improve accountability, reduce duplication and make individual progress easier to monitor.

## Sprint Meetings and Collaboration

Our group held regular meetings during Sprint 0 and Sprint 1 to discuss requirements, monitor progress and coordinate tasks.

Meeting records document the main discussions, decisions and follow-up actions.

During the 27 October meeting, the group agreed to organise project management through Notion, maintain project artefacts in GitHub and clarify individual responsibilities.

The meeting records provide supporting evidence of group communication and project coordination.

## Risk Management

A Risk Register was developed to identify potential technical, business and development risks associated with the project.

The risks include BPMN modelling inaccuracies, GitHub merge conflicts, certification verification failures, incorrect inventory availability and missed sprint deadlines.

Risks were assessed according to likelihood and impact, with mitigation strategies identified to reduce potential disruption.

The Risk Register will support risk monitoring and planning during subsequent sprints.
